LangChain, and Hugging Face SVG marks are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons)
and are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

TSMC, OpenAI, Amazon, xAI/Grok, and TBD positions use locally rendered initials rather than
unverified or third-party hosted logo files.
